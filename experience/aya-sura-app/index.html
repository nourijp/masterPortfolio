      Aya & Sura Dream World App Development

      Gamified iOS Learning App • Swift & Xcode • Supabase Backend

      Nov 2025 – Present

      Associated with Aya & Sura, LLC

      Project Overview

      Designed and led the development of a gamified iOS learning app built around original characters from the Aya & Sura universe. The app combines storytelling, character-driven visuals, and interactive flashcards to make language learning feel playful and intuitive.

      Design & Development

      I took the product from early ideation through design, testing, and production. This included defining the learning experience, designing the UI and UX, building interactive card mechanics, and ensuring the visuals translated cleanly from physical flashcards to a digital format.

      I collaborated with illustrators and editors, tested multiple design variations for usability and engagement, and refined the experience through repeated iteration.

      Technical Implementation

      On the technical side, I worked with Swift and Xcode to create functional app prototypes, integrated Supabase as the backend for content and data management, and coordinated system components so the app could scale alongside physical products and future content updates.

      The result was a cohesive learning product that bridged physical and digital experiences while staying true to the brand's visual identity and educational goals.
